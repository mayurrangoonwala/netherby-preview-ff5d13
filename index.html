The house

Your base for everything Niagara.

Set among open farmland, this two-bedroom country home is yours alone. Spend the day at the Falls, the wineries or the beaches, then come back to a garden view, a big sky and real quiet.

Inside there's a king bedroom on the main floor, a twin room upstairs, and a full kitchen with a gas stove and double oven. The laundry room means you can pack light. Starlink high-speed internet makes a working week here easy, and stays of 28 nights or more are welcome.

    • Park on the gravel drive to the right of the main entrance only.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    How does check-in work?

    It's self check-in with a smart lock. Your door code arrives before your stay. Check-in is from 4:00 pm and checkout is by 11:00 am.

    Where do I park?

    Parking is free, on the gravel drive to the right of the main entrance. Please keep vehicles off the back of the property.

    What's the cancellation policy?

    Full refund up to 7 days before check-in. 50% up to 48 hours before. No refund after that.

    Is the internet good enough to work?

    Yes. The house runs on Starlink high-speed internet, and plenty of guests work from here.

    Do you take longer stays?

    Yes. Stays of 28 nights or more are welcome, so ask about a monthly rate.

    Are there cameras?

    There are exterior cameras at the front door and around the outside of the property. None face into the home, and there are none inside.
